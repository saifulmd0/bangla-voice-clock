# Settings window and brand

Scope: the macOS settings window (Operate mode), the app icon and the menu-bar logo.
Audience: Bangladeshi Mac users at work, families and elders, fans of the 1998 app, people who rely on hearing the time.
Task: see when the clock will speak today, change how and when, hear it, and leave.
Constraints: native-feeling controls; Bangla first, English optional; large, high-contrast text for elders; credits always reachable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window owns the day as an alpona ring: today's schedule drawn, not listed. It refuses the category default of a grey grouped list under a clock-face icon.

OWN-WORLD: Rice-white line (#F6F0E4) drawn on fired-earth terracotta (#9C3D26) for the dial, logo and icon; turmeric (#E2A12B) marks now; leaf green (#2F6B3F) is the one control accent; soot ink (#231A16) text on a warm rice ground in light, an earth-dark ground in dark. Hind Siliguri for Bangla and Latin. Fingertip dot rows are the only ornament, and only on the dial.

STORY: The user understands at a glance when the clock speaks today, trusts it to stay quiet when asked, and sets it once.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A terracotta band holds a 240px alpona dial: a 24-hour ring with noon at the top, six day-part arcs labelled ভোর to রাত, one rice-white dot per scheduled announcement, quiet hours left unpainted, a turmeric dot at now; the time and next announcement sit inside; Speak now sits under the band. Native grouped rows follow at one generous type size.

FORM: Alpona Dial, position 4 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key a0750fff. Raises: every dot is data (ikeda); muted turns dots to outlines, never hides them (ticket wallet); one generous row size ranked by weight (timetable); the dial alone owns the header (saville).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
